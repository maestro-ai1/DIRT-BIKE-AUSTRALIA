# Keyword Strategy: Electric Dirt Bike Australia

All figures are from the Semrush AU export (25 Sep 2026) in `electric dirt bikes/`. Full page-by-page allocation: `docs/seo-keyword-allocation.md`.

## Rule
- Transactional = main keyword of a page (title + H1), product tags, product Q&A
- Commercial = primary keywords, categories, sub-categories, products
- Informational = blog posts and blog tags (`src/config/blog-seo.ts`)
- Navigational = FAQ (`FAQ_SEO` in `src/config/seo.ts`)
