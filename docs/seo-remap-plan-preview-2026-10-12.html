1. The golden rule used

Every keyword has exactly one owning page, and the intent label decides where it goes.
  1. [T] Transactional = the page's Main keyword: title (front-loaded) + H1 + meta, plus the buy/CTA block and price/FAQ answers.
  2. [C] Commercial = Primary and Secondary keywords: H2/H3 headings, intro, product-grid copy, product tags and Q&A.
  3. [I] Informational = blog posts and tags only; each post links to its owning money page with that page's keyword as anchor text.
  4. [N] Navigational = brand page and FAQ (delivery, warranty, stock location).

Anti-stuffing limits (every page): 1 main + at most 1 primary in the title; main phrase in the H1 once; each H2 carries one keyword; at most 4 H2 keywords + 4 FAQ keywords + 5 once-only body keywords per page; no exact phrase more than once per 100 words of prose; no keyword lists, no hidden text; spelling variants and awkward word orders are never written deliberately.
